Dr. Anandraj V. S.

“Business networking should never be about casual contact collection. At BusiHunt, we build structured pipelines and trusted circles, fostering ecosystems that act as active, powerful sales forces for our regional partners.”

Dr. Anandraj V. S. is a seasoned business strategist and industrial mentor with over twenty years of leadership experience in corporate development and ecosystem architecture. Recognizing the need for structured business matchmaking for MSMEs in emerging cities, he founded BusiHunt. Under his guidance, the organization has developed localized chapter roundtables and bi‑weekly metrics to help members trade, expand capacities, and unlock referral revenues.
